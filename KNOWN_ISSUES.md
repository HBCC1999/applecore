## Known Bugs (reported):
- (unresolved) Dynamic FPS not climbing back to DEFAULT_FPS:
After Dynamic FPS is set to False, the fps value climbs back to hardcoded value of 60, NOT the DEFAULT_FPS value.

- (unresolved) Snake body looks distorted a bit at fps other than 60.


## Issues/Inconsistencies:
- (unresolved) Dynamic FPS not functional on Desktops: Desktop PCs are always powered in, and powered_in
state ignores Dynamic FPS conditions, and defaults to 60 frames.
- (unresolved) No sign of Settings Page: There is no information or visual icon for user to click on specific area to open Settings Page.
